VIAPP extracts identity, matrix, layout and custom-key data and groups it into USB-ID shards. Menus are represented only by a capability flag. This is data; no VIA application source is included. Reproduce with scripts/sync-via-catalog.mjs and the above revision. See LICENSE.txt for redistribution terms.
